# Additional Methods

Sensitivity analyses retained the original matched allocation. Fibrosis
analyses used pairs with fibrosis recorded for both procedures and compared
models with and without categorical fibrosis adjustment. Models adjusted
for year and lesion size, then for the complete pretreatment covariate set.
The fully adjusted model without fibrosis was also fitted in the original
matched cohort. Lesion size was examined continuously using the archived
four-degree-of-freedom B-spline interaction model, with a joint Wald test
of its four treatment-by-size interaction coefficients. Standard errors
were clustered by matched set.

An exploratory interaction analysis compared two high-volume operators
(approximately 300 ESDs annually and more than 10 years of experience)
with lower-volume trained operators (approximately 50–100 ESDs annually
and 5 years of experience). Groups were defined by the study team and
treated as fixed operator characteristics. The model adjusted for all
pretreatment covariates and operator fixed effects. A sensitivity analysis
excluded the pooled operator stratum. Procedure duration was analysed
directly, adjusting for year and a four-degree-of-freedom B-spline in
specimen area. Size-specific effects came from one interaction model.
Matching with calipers of 0.40 and 0.60 SD of the logit propensity score
was evaluated as an exploratory sensitivity, with balance diagnostics
reported alongside the estimates. All tests were two-sided.

# Figure Legends

**Continuous-size interaction.** Adjusted difference in dissection speed
between ATRACT and conventional traction across lesion size in the original
matched cohort. The line and pointwise 95% confidence intervals reproduce
the estimates in Table S9 archived with v1.0.0. The overall interaction
p-value comes from a joint Wald chi-square test with 4 degrees of freedom,
using matched-set clustered covariance. The dashed line marks 50 mm for
orientation; it does not identify an estimated biological threshold.

**Operator-volume effects.** Adjusted differences in dissection speed in
high-volume and lower-volume trained operator groups, estimated from a
single treatment-by-volume interaction model. Adjustment includes the
pretreatment covariates and operator fixed effects. Confidence intervals
use matched-set clustered standard errors. Counts refer to matched pairs
within each operator-volume group.
